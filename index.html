Approach

Subtle by design. Rigorous by default.

Dianome is being shaped around the practical realities of genomic medicine: reliable methods, clear interpretation, data responsibility, and collaboration between scientific and clinical stakeholders.

The company is early, focused, and open to discussions with partners who are building evidence-based genomic medicine programs in Finland, the Nordics, and beyond.
